Privacy Policy

Finanzcockpit · last updated September 2026

What this application is

Finanzcockpit is a private, single-user application. It is operated by one

individual for the sole purpose of keeping a personal household budget and net

worth record. It is not offered to, marketed to, or usable by anyone else.

Whose data is processed

Only the data of the operator, who is also the sole account holder of every

connected bank account. There are no other users and no third-party data

subjects.

What data is accessed

Through the Enable Banking API, and only for accounts the operator has

personally linked and authorised:

  • account identifiers, account names and currencies
  • account balances
  • transaction records: date, amount, currency, description and counterparty

Access is read-only. The application cannot initiate payments.

Where the data is stored

In a single SQLite database file on the operator's own computer. The data is

not uploaded to any server, not stored in any cloud service, and not

transmitted to any third party.

Who the data is shared with

Nobody. There are no analytics, no advertising, no data sales, no processors

other than Enable Banking, which acts as the regulated account information

service provider enabling the bank connection.

How long the data is kept

Until the operator deletes the database file. There is no retention

requirement and no backup held elsewhere.

Legal basis and consent

Processing rests on the operator's own explicit consent, given separately to

each bank through that bank's authorisation flow. Consent is time-limited by

the bank (typically a maximum of 180 days) and must be renewed. It can be

withdrawn at any time, either in the bank's own online banking or by

unlinking the account in the Enable Banking Control Panel.

Rights

As the sole data subject is also the operator, all rights of access,

rectification and erasure are exercised directly by deleting or editing the

local database.

Contact

Enquiries: the email address registered as the data protection contact for

this application in the Enable Banking Control Panel.
